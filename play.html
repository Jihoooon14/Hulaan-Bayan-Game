Paano maglaro

Pick. Guess. Discover.

  1. Choose one of six Filipino categories, or play them all.
  2. Guess letters to reveal the word. Spaces and hyphens are already shown.
  3. Madali gives 6 chances, Katamtaman 5, and Mahirap 4. A wrong letter uses one chance.
  4. Correct guesses earn 10 points plus combo bonuses. Solve a word for 50 more points.
  5. Use one hint per word for 5 points. Scores never go below zero.

Bagong salita skips a word. Changing your challenge starts a new score. Scores last until you reload or leave this page.
