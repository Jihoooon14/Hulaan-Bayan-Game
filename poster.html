Game description

Hulaan Bayan is a Filipino-themed word-guessing game based on Hangman. Choose a category and difficulty, then guess letters to reveal the word before your chances run out. Correct guesses earn points and combos, while a hint can help you solve the puzzle. Six categories bring Filipino food, games, festivals, places, culture, and heroes into every challenge.

Game concept

Our starting point is the GROUP 5 Advanced Hangman project. We transformed it with Filipino vocabulary, local messages, Philippine-inspired colors, and a bamboo-style scaffold. We chose Filipino culture to connect a familiar game with our everyday traditions and heritage. Category selection, three difficulty levels, hints, and combo scoring give Hulaan Bayan its own identity.

Filipino theme

From adobo to bayanihan, the words celebrate Filipino life. Blue, red, gold, the sun motif, and bamboo-inspired artwork reflect the Philippines.
