Game description

A Filipino take on Hangman. Uncover the hidden word one letter at a time before your chances run out.

  • Choose from six Filipino categories.
  • Play at three difficulty levels.
  • Earn points, build combos, and use hints.

Game concept

Built on the GROUP 5 Advanced Hangman project, with Filipino vocabulary, local messages, and Philippine-inspired artwork.

A familiar word challenge becomes a celebration of our culture.
